About Us

Welcome to BTD Battles Stats

BTD Battles Stats (B1Data) is a dedicated community-driven analytics and statistical tracking platform created for competitive players of Bloons TD Battles. Our hub provides easy access to public leaderboard standings, historical match statistics, clan tracking, and individual player profile lookups.

Our Mission

The goal of this platform is to make competitive tracking accessible and simple. Whether you are analyzing weekly leaderboard movements, viewing past performance trends, or looking up specific player badges, BTD Battles Stats delivers concise data insights without requiring complicated software or forced user logins.
